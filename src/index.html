About

I’m an audio engineer and music producer based in Nashville, TN. I’ve been working in the industry for more than 8 years, in the studio and on stage, delivering sonic excellence and good vibes wherever I can.

I pride myself on my ability to adapt, learn, and grow in pretty much every situation, but I specialize in mixing and producing alternative, pop, and rock music. I also moonlight as a front of house and playback engineer, and have worked on a few specialized recording studio builds as a consulting audio engineer.

My philosophy is not controversial: your music should sound great, whatever that means to you. I believe anyone can get to that point without breaking the budget or putting themselves in rooms with people that don't understand the vision. If that sounds like the right attitude, head over to the Credits & Services page to see if we’d be a good fit, and shoot me a message.

Private Music Production & Mixing Lessons

One-on-one, project-based lessons for high-school students, aspiring college audio majors, independent artists, and adult learners in Nashville.

My goal is to help students understand what they are hearing, use their tools intentionally, and build a creative process they can eventually carry on without me.

How Lessons Work

We start with a free 20-minute phone or video consultation and a short intake form. The first lesson includes an informal assessment and a practical hands-on activity.

From there, we build a learning direction around essential audio concepts and a real project or clearly defined goal. Students operate the DAW for most of the lesson; my job is to demonstrate, guide, ask useful questions, and help them understand their choices.

FAQ

What ages do you teach?

I primarily teach students age 14 and older. Younger students may be considered depending on their interests, maturity, and previous musical experience.

Does a student need previous experience?

No. Complete beginners are welcome, as are experienced musicians who are new to recording or production.

Which software do you teach?

I teach Ableton Live and Pro Tools directly. Most recording, production, and mixing principles also transfer to other DAWs.

Does a student need their own computer or equipment?

Not to begin. Students can use the studio system during lessons. Regular access to a computer and DAW will become helpful as they progress.

Can students bring their own music?

Yes. Students are encouraged to bring original songs, recordings, mixes, demos, and unfinished projects. I can also provide exercises and practice material.

What is the cancellation policy?

At least 24 hours' notice is required to reschedule without charge. Complete payment, cancellation, and studio policies are provided before enrollment.
